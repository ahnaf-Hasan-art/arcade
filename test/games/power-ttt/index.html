Power Tic-Tac-Toe
ROUND 001
Current turn
💣 Bomb — pick one of your symbols on the board. Destroys all unshielded symbols in its 8 adjacent cells. Your chosen symbol survives.

🛡️ Shield — protects all of your symbols currently on the board for the next 2 full rounds. Blocks Bomb, Traitor, Clear Row and Clear Column, does not block Poison, Gravity, or Swap.

☠️ Poison — mark one of your symbols. At end of round +2 it destroys symbols orthogonally adjacent (↑↓←→) to it — ignoring Shield. At end of round +3 it hits the next ring further out in those same 4 directions, then the effect ends. Diagonals are always safe.

🎭 Traitor — pick one of your own symbols, then pick one adjacent opponent symbol (any of its 8 neighbors) to convert to your symbol. Blocked by Shield.

🚧 Clear Row — pick any cell. Destroys every unshielded symbol in that entire row, yours and opponents' alike.

🧹 Clear Column — pick any cell. Destroys every unshielded symbol in that entire column, yours and opponents' alike.

🪐 Gravity — pick any row. Every symbol in it falls straight down through its own column until it hits the bottom or another symbol. Shield/Poison travel with their symbol. Can complete a line for any player, not just you.

🔄 Swap — pick any two occupied cells on the board (yours or opponents') and swap their symbols. Can complete a line for whoever ends up with the winning symbols.
