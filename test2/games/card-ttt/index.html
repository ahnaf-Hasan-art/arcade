How to play
Play a card from your hand adjacent to an existing symbol on the board. In Combined movement mode, straight cards need any orthogonal neighbor (up/down/left/right) and diagonal cards need any diagonal neighbor. In Directional movement mode each card only works along one single axis — Horizontal, Vertical, "\" diagonal, or "/" diagonal — so you need a neighbor specifically along that axis. Get the required number of the same symbol in a row to win — it doesn't matter who placed each one, only who completes the line.

💣 Bomb — pick one of your own cells; destroys unshielded symbols in the 8 adjacent cells.
🛡️ Shield — protects all your current cells for 2 rounds.
☠️ Poison — mark one of your cells; it detonates in stages over the following rounds, ignoring shields.
🎭 Traitor — pick your own cell, then an adjacent unshielded opponent cell to convert.
🚧 Clear Row / 🧹 Clear Column — destroys unshielded symbols in that row/column.
🪐 Gravity — everything in a row falls down its own column.
🔄 Swap — swap any two occupied cells, symbol and owner together.

If none of your cards can legally be played, you must discard your whole hand and pass.
